Welcome to my blog

This is my first post. I just set this thing up and it feels like the old internet again. No tracking, no cookies popup, no newsletter signup modal. Just words on a screen.

I will be writing here about whatever I feel like. Expect the unexpected, or maybe just expect the very expected. Who knows.

Why a blog?

Because social media is temporary. Posts get buried. Algorithms decide who sees what. Here, it is just me and whoever types in the URL. The way it should be.

What to expect

  • Random thoughts
  • Things I learned
  • Occasional rants
  • Maybe some code

That is about it. See you around.
